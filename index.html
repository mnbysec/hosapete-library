Our Building

Right by the District Stadium

The City Central Library stands in the heart of Hosapete, a short walk from the District Stadium. Step in to read the day's papers, study in the quiet hall, or borrow from the Kannada and English shelves.

It is the main library of the Department of Public Libraries in Vijayanagara district, supported by seven neighbourhood branches across the city.

About the Library

A bright civic reading room in Hosapete

The City Central Library serves the people of Hosapete and the surrounding Vijayanagara district under the Department of Public Libraries, Government of Karnataka. The doors are open to all — walk in to read, or enrol to borrow from the shelves.

The collection spans Kannada and English literature, school and competitive-exam study material, reference works, and a daily newspaper and periodicals counter. A children's corner and a quiet study hall give students and lifelong readers a calm place to work.

You'll find the library a short walk from the District Stadium in the heart of the city — and a short drive from the Hampi heritage landscape on the Tungabhadra.

Become a Member

Join the library

Membership lets you borrow books from the City Central Library and its branches. Enrol at the membership counter — it only takes a few minutes.

Documents required

  • Any government-issued identity card
